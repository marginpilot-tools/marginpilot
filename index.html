Built for real-world pricing decisions

MarginPilot is an early-stage digital spreadsheet product. We are validating calculations and usability before release. The calculator is not connected to any marketplace and does not automatically retrieve fee schedules.

Platform fees, taxes, returns and other costs vary. Always verify your inputs.
